## Full description (4000 max)

Selling your phone? Show buyers it works, before they ask.

PhoneProof runs a quick self-test of the phone's hardware: screen, touch, speaker, microphone, cameras, flash, buttons, sensors, battery and more. When you're done, the phone signs the results with its built-in security chip. Change one thing in the report and the signature breaks, so buyers know the results came from this phone and weren't edited.

HOW IT WORKS
• Run the check. Most tests run on their own. A few ask you a simple yes or no, like "did you hear the tone?"
• Get your report. You see what passed, what needs a look, and anything you skipped.
• Share it. Add the report card to your listing photos, or send a link. Buyers open the link in any browser, for free, no app needed.

MEETING A BUYER?
Meet-up mode proves the report comes from the phone in front of them. The buyer's phone shows a code, your phone scans it and answers with a fresh signed report in seconds. It works offline, so a weak signal at a café is fine.

BUYING A USED PHONE?
Open the seller's link or scan their QR code. PhoneProof checks the phone's signature, Google's hardware certificate chain and that the report came from the real PhoneProof app.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• A self-test report, signed by this device. It shows what the phone measured and what the seller confirmed, and says which is which.
• Skipped tests are shown as "not tested", never as passed.
• It can't check original parts, water damage, carrier or account locks, or whether a phone is reported stolen. Check the IMEI with your country's stolen-phone service.

PRIVATE
• Tests run on your phone. Camera and microphone are used only during their tests. Nothing is recorded or saved.
• Reports stay on your phone unless you choose to get a link. Links expire after 90 days.
• No account, no sign-up.

PhoneProof is free and supported by ads. There are never ads during the tests or in meet-up mode.
